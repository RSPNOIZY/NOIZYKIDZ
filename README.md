> ## The Mission
>
> NOIZYKIDZ is a music education platform built on one truth:
>
> **Sound is not the only door into music.**
>
> Vibration, pattern, colour, rhythm, and touch are equally valid entry points — and for many children, they are the *primary* ones. NOIZYKIDZ is built for the kids who were told music wasn't for them. It is Robert Stephen Plowman's **personal mission** — his heart brand.
>
> Every kid gets to hold the pen.

> ## Design Principles
>
> | Principle | What It Means |
> |---|---|
> | **Deaf-first** | If it doesn't work without hearing, it doesn't ship. Full stop. |
> | **Autism-calm** | No sensory ambush. Every interaction is predictable, adjustable, and opt-in. |
> | **Haptic-native** | Rhythm lives in the body before it lives in the ear. Vibration is the foundation. |
> | **Consent-native** | Children's data, likeness, and creative output are protected by the same standards as adults. No exceptions. Ever. |
> | **Every kid plays** | Not a feature. A constitutional requirement. |
